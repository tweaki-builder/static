About Lush

This is who we are

Creators of pioneering beauty products such as the fizzing bath bomb, shower jellies, and solid shampoo bars, we place emphasis on fresh ingredients like organic fruits and vegetables, fight against animal testing, and combat over-packaging by developing products that can be sold ‘naked’ to the customer without any packaging.

OUR LATEST CAMPAIGN

In Solidarity with Gaza

We share the anguish that millions of people feel seeing the images of starving people in Gaza, Palestine. Like the rest of the world, we struggle to find ways we can help whilst the Israeli government is preventing urgent humanitarian assistance from entering Gaza. One thing Lush can currently send into Gaza is our love and a strong message that we stand in solidarity.

A brief timeline of Lush

1995

We Believe statement written

1999

A Lush bath bomb is exhibited in the Tate Art Gallery

2001

First Lush patent granted – for Bubble Bars

2002

The Queen accepts a Street Party bath bomb

2003

B Never Too Busy To Be Beautiful opened its doors

2004

Number 10 in The Sunday Times’ Best Companies To Work For

2006

Lush dumps 2 tonnes of manure at European Parliament

2009

Lush launched Charity Pot, Knot Wraps and the Black Pot scheme

2009

Lush launches first Spa in Poole

2011

Lush becomes the first retail company to pay the London Living Wage

2012

The Lush Prize is launched to support animal-free testing

2012

A ‘live human testing’ window in Regent Street is installed as a protest

2013

Lush sets a new Guinness World Records

2014

The Self Preserving range is launched everywhere

2014

The Experimenter video hits the headlines

2014

Lush wins its court case against Amazon, for infringing its trademark

2015

Awarded the Fair Tax Mark

2016

Lush’s website switches off in support of #KeepItOn

2017

The EBT is established, holding 10% of the company for the benefit of Lush staff

2018

The first Naked Concept shop opens its doors in Milan, Italy

2017

Lush commits to the Real Living Wage for all UK retail staff in April

2019

Lush Lens is launched, reaching over 1million scans in just a few months

2019

The first carbon positive packaging at Lush arrives, by boat… Cork Pots
